Q: What would be the best data structure to model the behavior of the supply basket? Justify your answer and explain how it would be used.
A: This behaviour would be modeled with a stack, as stacks are optimized for data that is retrieved in the same order that it is stored. When items are added to the supply basket, they are added to the top of the stack (Stack.Push(value)), and when they are removed, they are likewise removed from the top of the stack (Stack.Pop()). If there are no items in the stack, the basket is empty (Stack.Count == 0).

Q: What is the time complexity of adding new seeds to the supply basket?
A: O(1); there are no factors that affect the complexity of adding items to a stack.

Q: What is the time complexity of removing seeds from the supply basket?
A: O(1); there are no factors that affect the complexity of removing items from a stack.

Q: What is the time complexity of checking to see if the supply basket is empty?
A: O(1); there are no factors that affect the complexity of checking the size of a stack.
